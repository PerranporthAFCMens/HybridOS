Delete your data

Last updated 11 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data we hold about you at any time.

How to ask

  1. Email hello@hybridone.co.uk from the address you signed up with. Put “Delete my data” in the subject line.
  2. Tell us which gym you joined, if you remember.
  3. We will confirm it is you, then delete your account and personal details within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What happens

  • Your account, contact details, address and emergency contact are removed.
  • Your membership ends. Any active membership is cancelled first.
  • Some records may have to be kept where the law requires it, such as payment and accounting records. These are kept only for that purpose.

Signed in with Facebook or Google?

Deleting your data here removes it from HybridOne. To also remove HybridOne’s access from your Facebook or Google account, remove the app in that account’s settings.
