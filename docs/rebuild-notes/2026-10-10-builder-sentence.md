# Report builder: sentence bar and side panel (2026-10-10)

First step of the reports redesign (see the owner-approved mock-ups).

- The question is now a sentence at the top: "Look at [dataset] and show [Total Amount] by [Charge date] for last 30 days". The three chips are real drop-downs and change the same settings as the boxes.
- "Start from a ready-made report" is one button that opens the 12 starting reports (was a big card).
- Group by, Values, Filters (and Columns in list mode) and the Fields list moved to a side panel on the right. On a phone the Fields come first, then the boxes.
- Nothing changes in the engine, data, downloads or saved reports.
- Browser checks: the drag checks scroll the target into view first, because the field list and the boxes are now stacked in one column.
- Still to do from the mock-ups: reports home (headline figures with change and trend), dashboards, phone bottom sheet, three-step export.
