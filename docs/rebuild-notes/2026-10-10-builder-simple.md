# Report builder: simpler, and one look across the reports (2026-10-10)

Owner feedback: too busy and complicated, typefaces inconsistent, hard to know how to use, the paper report should not be on screen, and the headline tiles differ between report tabs.

- One column. The report is one sentence: "Show [Total Amount] by [Charge date] from [Payments]". "Every row, as a list" is the first choice in "Show", so there is no separate list/summarise switch.
- The result is the chart (or a short table when listing). The paper report only appears in "Print report" (a dialog with a Print button and print styles).
- Downloads are three buttons: CSV, Excel, PDF. Saving a report opens a small dialog.
- Active filters show as chips under the sentence. Filters, columns and fields (the drag-and-drop boxes) are tucked behind "Filters, columns and fields".
- One typeface everywhere in the builder (the app font); the mono font is gone from figures.
- Headline tiles are the same on every reports tab and in the builder: 2 columns on a phone, 4 from 700px, same height, label on top, figure in the app font.
