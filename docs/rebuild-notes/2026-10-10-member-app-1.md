# Member app, step 1: Today, Classes, Me (2026-10-10)

Owner decisions this step: members can swap or skip exercises in work their coach sends; what they record is a record not an obligation ("this isn't school": recommendations they can mark done or not, no "missed" counts). Whether a coach can reply is not decided yet.

Built, at `/next/#/m/today`, `#/m/classes`, `#/m/me` (new `src/member/`, `src/data/member.ts`):
- **Today:** one card on top (the soonest booked class or PT session, else "book your next class", else a welcome), "Book another" suggestions, the weekly ring (only for members whose plan includes the gym, or who have logged), and the next PT session.
- **Classes:** a strip of 14 days, tap Book or Cancel. Cancelling asks first. Full classes say Full.
- **Me:** name, gym, plan and what it includes, sign out, switch gym, and a link to the classic app for the things not rebuilt yet.
- Tabs come from the plan (`includes_classes`, `includes_open_gym`, `includes_pt`): a member without classes has no Classes tab.
- Booking uses the same database functions as the classic app (`member_class_schedule`, `member_book_class`, `member_cancel_class`, `get_class_booking_options`). A class outside the plan shows the options but online drop-in payment is not connected (as in the classic app), so it says to ask the gym.
- Real members are still sent to the classic `member.html`. Owners and admins can preview with "Preview new member app" in the sidebar; the cutover changes where members land.

Not built yet: Train (today's workout, logging, Left/Right/Both, pushed workouts), PT detail and past sessions, personal bests, community/groups/Strava, membership page, join and onboarding.

## Me: change name, email and password (2026-10-10)
Same behaviour as the classic account menu: the name updates the profile row and the sign-in account; an email change sends a confirmation to the new address and the old one keeps working until it is confirmed (the existing `send-auth-email` function already has the email-change message); a password needs 8 or more characters, typed twice. Not included: date of birth, gender and profile photo (still in the classic account menu).
