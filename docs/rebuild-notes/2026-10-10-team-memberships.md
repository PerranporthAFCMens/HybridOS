# Team memberships

Owners, admins, staff and coaches can hold a membership like anyone else, so they can use the member app.
- **Members** now lists the team too, marked Owner / Admin / Staff / Coach, with a "Show" filter (everyone, members only, team only).
- Opening a team person shows the usual "Assign membership" form (the owner picks the plan). Member lifecycle dates (joined, left) are hidden for the team because they only apply to members.
- Member app › Me tells a team login with no membership how to get one.
- Reports and Today still count members only. No SQL: the database already lets staff roles create memberships for anyone in the gym.
