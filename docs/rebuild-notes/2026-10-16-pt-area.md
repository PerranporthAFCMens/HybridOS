# Clients tab in the staff app (2026-10-16)

Step 2 of the PT area. Needs `20261016120000_pt_clients.sql` and `20261016121000_pt_clients_replies.sql` run first (see `2026-10-16-pt-clients.md`); until they are, the list shows "Could not load your clients".

## What a PT sees (the **Clients** tab in `/s/`, the staff app)
- **Clients**: one list, clients who need a reply first. One line each ("Finished a workout, needs your reply", "2 workouts waiting for them", "Last workout 2 days ago", "Quiet for 3 weeks", "Nothing set yet"). Search appears once there are more than five. **Add client** picks from the gym's active members. A member also appears by themselves once the PT has a booked or finished PT session with them.
- **A client** (`/s/clients/:userId`): their name, then the same Training panel the owner sees on a member's file: **Send a workout**, what is waiting, and what they have done with a one-tap reply. Nothing new was built for that part.
- Owners and admins see **All clients** (every active member); the database already lets them see everyone.
