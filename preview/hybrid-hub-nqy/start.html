Hybrid Hub START

Small group training. More support. More confidence.

New to training

No experience needed. Just START.

Hybrid Hub START is designed for those who want to train at Hybrid Hub but would benefit from a little more support and a smaller training environment.

You'll follow the same Hybrid Hub programming as our regular classes, but delivered in a small group of just 4 to 6 people, giving you more individual coaching, guidance and confidence throughout each session.

With 2 coached sessions per week and full gym access included, START gives you the structure and support to learn, progress and become more comfortable in the gym.
