Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

HybridOne is the membership system your gym uses to run sign-ups, classes and memberships. This page explains what is stored about you and who can see it.

Who is responsible for your data

The gym you join decides how your membership information is used. HybridOne provides the system and looks after it securely on the gym’s behalf.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and password (stored securely, never readable).
  • Your mobile number, address, date of birth and emergency contact, when you give them while joining.
  • The terms and waiver you sign, your signature, and the date and time you signed.
  • Your membership, class bookings and attendance.

We do not store card or bank details. Payments, when switched on, are taken on the payment provider’s own secure form.

Why we use it

To set up and run your membership, let you book classes, keep a record of what you agreed to, and send you messages from your gym, such as your signed copy of the terms.

Who can see it

Other members can never see your details. Only the team at your gym (owners, admins and staff) can see them, and a gym owner can choose whether the team sees mobile numbers and addresses.

Sharing with other companies

We do not share your information with third parties, and we never sell it. The only time information goes to another company is when you choose it, by giving your consent inside the app. Examples are connecting a service such as Strava or paying through Stripe, and there may be others. You are always asked first, and you can disconnect at any time.

Who looks after it for us

Your data is stored with Supabase, and our emails are sent through Resend. They only handle data to provide those services for us, and they may not use it for anything else.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a member, and afterwards only as long as the gym or the law requires. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data held about you. Email hello@hybridone.co.uk or ask your gym. You can also see how to delete your data.
